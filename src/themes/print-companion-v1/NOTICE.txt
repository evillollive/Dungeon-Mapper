Original monochrome 32-unit line drawings in art.ts. No external artwork,
fonts, downloads, textures or raster atlases are required for the tile kit.
Material patterns use fixed map coordinates and do not depend on page offsets.
Unknown material versions use flagstone, without changing the saved material ID.

Version 1.1.0 adds thin contours where printed floor materials meet. Matching
materials have no extra edges; each transition is drawn once, inside the
higher-priority material (wood, then earth, then flagstone). Existing water,
wall and doorway boundaries are unchanged. The owner approved this print-only
treatment on September 28, 2026; physical-paper acceptance remains separate.

Version 1.2.0 integrates the stair treads with a wide directional arrow:
five crossbars meet both sloping sides and the central shaft. Up and down
are vertically mirrored. This owner-selected print-only revision is pending
final in-context visual approval. Color artwork and stair-link behavior
are unchanged.

The approved Folio furnishing and token sources retain their own manifests
and notices. Furnishing print strokes use neutral charcoal; their approved
paths and color-mode paints are unchanged. Legacy tokens use the same party shield, NPC seal and hostile
hexagon. Their bundled glyphs remain; emoji-only glyphs use a label initial
in print because color emoji fonts cannot guarantee monochrome output.

The three original encounter compositions are in src/utils/launchSamples.ts
under the same license. They reuse bundled themes and furnishing/token art.
They do not replace the wilderness/starship adaptation kits in ART-05.
