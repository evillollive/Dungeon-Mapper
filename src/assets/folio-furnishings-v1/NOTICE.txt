Twenty-four original top-down SVG furnishings authored for Dungeon Mapper with
GitHub Copilot assistance: table, chair, bed, shelf, crate, barrel, altar,
rubble, round table, bench, stool, writing desk, chest, wardrobe, weapon rack,
brazier, sarcophagus, supply sacks, boulder, fern, shrub, campfire, bedroll,
and tent. No third-party artwork, fonts or textures are included.

Version 1.1.0 adds sixteen sources. The original eight SVG sources, their
stable IDs, default scales and the 1.x rendering contract are unchanged.

Version 1.1.1 strengthens monochrome silhouette strokes to at least 2.4 frame
units and interior strokes to at least 1 unit. Colored source artwork, path
geometry, IDs, scales and shadows are unchanged. This print-only adjustment
was requested and visually approved by the owner on September 28, 2026.
Physical-paper acceptance remains separate.

The .svg files are editable, authoritative path-only sources. catalog.ts
admits this deliberately restricted format and derives monochrome paths.
manifest.ts records stable IDs, source fingerprints and nominal sizing.

All artwork uses a centered 64-unit square frame. A scale of 1 is one
map cell; default scales vary by object, and are saved on placement.
Frames and shadows are decorative, not collision or line-of-sight rules.
Cast shadows use a common map-space southeast offset and are suppressed
in print. Existing stamps and previously saved IDs are not replaced.
